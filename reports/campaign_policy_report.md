# Campaign Policy Report

This report is generated manually in the starter repository. Future versions should generate it automatically from pipeline outputs.

## Core question

Can we estimate which customers are influenced by the campaign, instead of only predicting who is likely to convert?

## Recommended policy framing

Use uplift scores to prioritize customers expected to have positive incremental response.

Do not treat the model as a replacement for randomized testing, business judgment, consent rules, or customer experience constraints.
